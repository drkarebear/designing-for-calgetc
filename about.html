Why this exists

About

Designing for Cal-GETC is an independent faculty-facing translation layer: not a student planner, not an approval authority, and not a replacement for your articulation officer or curriculum committee.

Important

Independent faculty resource

Designing for Cal-GETC is independently created and maintained. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the California Community Colleges Chancellor’s Office, and it is not an approval authority. Official statewide documents and local curriculum processes control when they differ from this guide.

Accessibility feedback may be sent to karencrozer@gmail.com.

The problem

Faculty course designers may need to consult Cal-GETC standards, Title 5/COR rules, reviewer notes, CCN, C-ID, UC transferability guidance, ASSIST, and local processes at the same time.

The design principle

Put the faculty task first, keep source boundaries visible, and never make an interpretation look like a statewide mandate.
