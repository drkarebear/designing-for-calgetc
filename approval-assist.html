Approval & ASSIST

Design is only part of the process. This page explains where Cal-GETC approval is published, when an approval becomes effective, and what happens during re-review or phase-out.

1. Statewide review happens annually

UC and CSU jointly review CCC courses submitted for Cal-GETC. Decisions are announced in spring to articulation officers and updated in ASSIST each academic year.

2. Approval has an effective term

A successfully submitted course generally begins carrying its Cal-GETC area certification in the fall term of the academic year after submission, provided the course is active or will be active as specified in the standards.

3. ASSIST is the published record

After approval, the course appears on the college's Cal-GETC course list in ASSIST. Certification is honored when the course was on the approved list for that college when the student completed it.

4. Existing approvals can be re-reviewed

If a course is later found not to meet current standards, it may be scheduled for phase-out while remaining on the approved list for at least two academic years, giving the CCC time to revise and resubmit when appropriate.

5. A denial can be revised and resubmitted

The standards say detailed reasons for denial are provided to the CCC, which may modify the COR and submit it in the following cycle.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards Version 1.4, §§4–5.1.2.

ASSIST term

Effective Begin

The year term when an approval or transfer area begins to apply. A blank Effective Begin indicates applicability back to the beginning of the course.

ASSIST term

Effective End

The year term when an approval ends; that end term itself is excluded. A blank Effective End indicates ongoing applicability.

Source: ASSIST, “Understanding Year Terms in ASSIST” (June 9, 2026).
