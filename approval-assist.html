Approval & ASSIST

See where approval appears, when it takes effect, and what happens after a denial or re-review.

1. Statewide review happens annually

UC and CSU review CCC Cal-GETC submissions each year. Decisions are published in ASSIST.

2. Approval has an effective term

An approved course carries Cal-GETC credit beginning with its published effective term.

3. ASSIST is the published record

Use ASSIST to confirm the approved area and the terms when that approval applies.

4. Existing approvals can be re-reviewed

An existing approval can be re-reviewed. If it no longer meets the standard, it may be phased out while the college revises and resubmits.

5. A denial can be revised and resubmitted

A denial includes reasons. The college can revise the COR and submit again in a later cycle.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards Version 1.4, §§4–5.1.2.

How ASSIST effective terms work
ASSIST term

Effective Begin

The year term when an approval or transfer area begins to apply. A blank Effective Begin indicates applicability back to the beginning of the course.

ASSIST term

Effective End

The year term when an approval ends; that end term itself is excluded. A blank Effective End indicates ongoing applicability.

Source: ASSIST, “Understanding Year Terms in ASSIST” (June 9, 2026).
