Areas & Standards

Pick the Cal-GETC area your course is intended to satisfy. Each guide starts with the official criteria, then translates them into course-design questions and places in the COR where the evidence can be made visible.

Cal-GETC at a glance

The pattern includes 11 courses totaling 34 semester or 45 quarter units. Area 1 has three subareas, Area 3 requires one Arts and one Humanities course, Area 4 requires two disciplines, and Area 5 requires physical science, biological science, and a laboratory component.
