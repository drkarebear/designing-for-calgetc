Areas & Standards

Pick the Cal-GETC area your course is intended to satisfy. Each guide starts with the official criteria, then translates them into course-design questions and places in the COR where the evidence can be made visible.

Areas 1, 3, and 5 have subareas.Open the overview when you need the relationship among subareas, or go directly to the course area you are designing.Use the design path →
Area 1

English Communication

3 courses9 semester / 12 quarter units

English composition, critical thinking and composition, and oral communication are three distinct required subareas.

Mathematical Concepts & Quantitative Reasoning

1 course3 semester / 4 quarter units

Mathematics and quantitative reasoning are the course’s primary purpose and content.

Area 3

Arts & Humanities

2 courses6 semester / 8 quarter units

Students complete one Arts course and one Humanities course; the two subareas have related but distinct criteria.

Open Area 3 overview →
Go directly to:3AArts3BHumanities
Area 4

Social & Behavioral Sciences

2 coursesTwo academic disciplines

Theory, disciplinary perspectives, and research methods focused on social and behavioral phenomena.

Open Area 4 guide →
Area 5

Physical & Biological Sciences

At least 2 courses7 semester / 9 quarter units

One physical science, one biological science, and a qualifying laboratory component associated with at least one of them.

Ethnic Studies

1 course3 semester / 4 quarter units

Ethnic Studies meeting at least three CSU or UC core competencies with appropriate disciplinary location.
