Biological science through scientific inquiry

Area 5B · Biological Science

Biological science grounded in core disciplinary concepts, evidence-based reasoning, experimental methodology, hypothesis testing, and theoretical principles.

Explore the standards:
Check My Cal-GETC Area →
At a glanceCal-GETC Standard

Area 5 requires one Biological Science course and one Physical Science course, with at least one associated laboratory.

Area 5B courses focus on core concepts of a biological science discipline and the practices of scientific inquiry.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§9.5–9.5.1.

What the course must make visibleCal-GETC Standard
  • Core concepts of a biological science discipline.
  • Experimental methodology, testing of hypotheses, investigation, and systematic questioning.
  • Evidence-based reasoning and fundamental theoretical principles rather than primarily recall of facts.
  • A course design appropriate to baccalaureate-level general education.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§9.5–9.5.1.

Does this course sound like a fit?Faculty Design Note

Usually a good fit when…

  • Students investigate biological concepts through scientific reasoning.
  • Hypothesis testing, evidence, and theory are visible in the course.
  • The course does more than survey facts and terminology.

Usually not a fit when…

  • Recall of facts, data, and events dominates the course.
  • The course is primarily applied or vocational rather than disciplinary science.
  • Scientific inquiry and theoretical principles are not visible in the COR.

Faculty Design Note: these are plain-language screening cues drawn from the official area criteria, not separate approval rules.

Make it visible in the CORReviewer Guidance

The statewide reviewer guidance emphasizes that reviewers should not have to infer missing criteria from the course title or discipline. Use the COR sections that best demonstrate the required content and rigor.

What the standard emphasizesWhere faculty can make it visible in the COR
Core biological science conceptsObjectives and content
Experimental methodology / hypothesis testingObjectives, content, assignments
Evidence-based reasoningAssignments and evaluation
Laboratory connection, if this is the lab scienceContent, hours, assignments, lab manual

Reviewer Guidance + Faculty Design Note: the table suggests where evidence can be made visible; it does not create new state-mandated COR fields.

Gray area / useful nuance

Area 5 is evaluated as a combined pattern: one 5A course, one 5B course, and at least one laboratory component meeting Area 5C. The total must satisfy the Area 5 unit minimum.
