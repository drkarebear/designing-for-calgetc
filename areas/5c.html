Laboratory practice connected to science content

Area 5C · Laboratory

A laboratory component connected to an approved physical or biological science course, using hands-on or validated simulation-based scientific investigation.

Explore the standards:
Check My Cal-GETC Area →
At a glanceCal-GETC Standard

At least one Area 5 science course must include a laboratory component.

The laboratory may be integrated into a 5A or 5B course or offered as a standalone lab associated by prerequisite or corequisite.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§9.5.2–9.5.3.

What the course must make visibleCal-GETC Standard
  • Hands-on or validated simulation-based manipulation of matter, equipment, and instrumentation.
  • Safe use of equipment and instruments relevant to the subject.
  • Experimental methodology and hypothesis testing connected to the principles taught in the related lecture course.
  • At least one unit for a standalone laboratory course.
  • A clearly identified laboratory manual in the COR.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§9.5.2–9.5.3.

Does this course sound like a fit?Faculty Design Note

Usually a good fit when…

  • The lab is directly related to an approved Area 5A or 5B course.
  • Students engage in authentic or validated simulated laboratory investigation.
  • The COR clearly identifies the lab relationship, units/hours, safety expectations, and lab manual.

Usually not a fit when…

  • The laboratory is unrelated to the corresponding lecture science.
  • The activity is demonstration-only or lacks meaningful student manipulation / investigation.
  • A standalone lab is below the minimum unit requirement or lacks the required prerequisite/corequisite relationship.

Faculty Design Note: these are plain-language screening cues drawn from the official area criteria, not separate approval rules.

Make it visible in the CORReviewer Guidance

The statewide reviewer guidance emphasizes that reviewers should not have to infer missing criteria from the course title or discipline. Use the COR sections that best demonstrate the required content and rigor.

What changed in Version 1.4?Version change

Version 1.4 added an example clarifying that a separate 1-unit corresponding physical science lab can combine with 3-unit lecture courses to satisfy the 7-semester-unit Area 5 minimum.

See tracked changes →

Gray area / useful nuance

A 3-semester-unit integrated lab science can satisfy the laboratory requirement as long as the full Area 5 pattern still reaches at least 7 semester units.
