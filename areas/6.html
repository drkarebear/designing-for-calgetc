Ethnic Studies core competencies

Area 6 · Ethnic Studies

Ethnic Studies courses meeting at least three of five CSU or UC core competencies, with appropriate Ethnic Studies prefixes or cross-listing.

At a glance

Cal-GETC Standard

One course · 3 semester or 4 quarter units.

A CCC course may be written with both CSU and UC Ethnic Studies core competencies in mind, but it must meet either the CSU or UC set. Meeting one is deemed to meet the other.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §9.6.

What the course must make visible

Cal-GETC Standard
  • An appropriate Ethnic Studies prefix or cross-listing with an Ethnic Studies-prefixed course.
  • At least three of the five applicable CSU or UC Ethnic Studies core competencies.
  • Substantive engagement with race, racism, racialization, ethnicity, power, and related concepts in one or more of the named populations/fields.
  • Theory and knowledge produced by Native American, African American, Asian American, and/or Latina/o/x American communities.
  • Critical analysis of intersections, histories, struggles, resistance, justice, solidarity, liberation, and anti-racist / anti-colonial thought and movements.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §9.6.

Does this course sound like a fit?

Faculty Design Note

Usually a good fit when…

  • The course is clearly situated in Ethnic Studies as a field, not merely about diversity in a broad sense.
  • At least three competencies are explicit and developed across the COR.
  • The course centers theory and knowledge produced by the named communities and fields.

Usually not a fit when…

  • The course lacks an appropriate Ethnic Studies prefix and is not cross-listed appropriately.
  • Race or diversity appears as one topic but does not organize the course through Ethnic Studies frameworks.
  • The COR cannot demonstrate at least three core competencies.

Faculty Design Note: these are plain-language screening cues drawn from the official area criteria, not separate approval rules.

Make it visible in the COR

Reviewer Guidance

The statewide reviewer guidance emphasizes that reviewers should not have to infer missing criteria from the course title or discipline. Use the COR sections that best demonstrate the required content and rigor.

What changed in Version 1.4?

Version change

Version 1.4 clarifies that Area 6 courses must meet either the CSU or UC core competencies and adds extra consideration during review for CCC Ethnic Studies courses that existed before the 2020 passage of AB 1460.

See tracked changes →

Gray area / useful nuance

The CSU and UC competency lists are closely aligned but not word-for-word identical. The site summarizes them for navigation; faculty should verify the current official competency language in Version 1.4 when drafting or revising a COR.
