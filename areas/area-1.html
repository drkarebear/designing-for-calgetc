Area 1 · English Communication

At a glance

Cal-GETC Standard

Three courses · 9 semester or 12 quarter units.

Area 1 requires one course in each of three distinct subareas: English Composition (1A), Critical Thinking and Composition (1B), and Oral Communication (1C).

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§2 and 9.1.

How Area 1 fits together

Faculty map
1A

English Composition

Academic writing, recursive process, rhetorical awareness, substantial reading, feedback, and scaffolded writing across major assignments.

 1B

Critical Thinking & Composition

Builds on 1A through critical reasoning, inquiry-driven research, source evaluation, and argumentative writing.

 1C

Oral Communication

Public speaking grounded in rhetorical theory, research, argument, ethics, live-audience practice, delivery, and critical listening.

Relationships faculty should notice

Cal-GETC Standard
  • 1A comes before 1B. Successful completion of Area 1A develops the reading and composition skills that are prerequisite to Area 1B.
  • All Area 1 courses are delivered in English.
  • 1C is not simply “communication” broadly. The standard centers foundational public speaking and a substantial focus on rhetorical principles.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§9 and 9.1.1–9.1.3.

Why use the Area 1 overview first?

Area 1 contains three required courses that do related but different work. If you are revising one course, use the subarea guide. If you are reviewing a sequence, degree map, or department curriculum, this overview helps keep the relationships visible without blending the criteria together.
