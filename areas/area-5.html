Two sciences plus a laboratory component

Area 5 · Physical & Biological Sciences

Area 5 requires physical science, biological science, and a laboratory component associated with at least one of them. Use this overview to see how 5A, 5B, and 5C work together.

At a glance

Cal-GETC Standard

At least two courses · 7 semester or 9 quarter units.

Area 5 requires one Physical Science course (5A), one Biological Science course (5B), and at least one laboratory component (5C) associated with one of those sciences.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §9.5.

5C does not necessarily mean a third course

Important distinction

The laboratory may be integrated into an approved 5A or 5B course, or it may be a standalone laboratory tied to the corresponding lecture through a prerequisite or corequisite. A standalone lab must be at least one semester or quarter unit.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§9.5.2–9.5.3.

How Area 5 fits together

Faculty map
5A

Physical Science

Core physical-science concepts, theory, evidence, experimental methodology, hypothesis testing, and scientific reasoning.

 5B

Biological Science

Core biological-science concepts, theory, evidence, experimental methodology, hypothesis testing, and scientific reasoning.

 5C

Laboratory

Hands-on or validated simulation-based investigation connected to an Area 5A or 5B science course.

Relationships faculty should notice

Cal-GETC Standard
  • Students need both a physical and a biological science.
  • At least one must include laboratory content.
  • The lab and lecture must be related. The standards connect laboratory methodology and hypothesis testing to principles presented in the lecture course.
  • The COR matters for the lab relationship. An integrated lecture/lab must show the lab in the COR, while a standalone lab must identify the relevant prerequisite or corequisite.
  • Lab science courses must identify a lab manual in the COR.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards v1.4, §§9.5–9.5.3.

Think in combinations, not isolated boxes

Area 5 is easiest to understand as a package: one 5A course + one 5B course + a qualifying lab attached to at least one of them, with the total area unit requirement satisfied. The individual guides help with each component; this page helps faculty see whether the pieces actually fit together.
