Related COR / Title 5 context

Cal-GETC review does not replace the broader COR requirements. Before local submission, your college must also apply current Title 5 and local curriculum requirements.

  • The COR includes the required statewide core elements.
  • Representative inclusive pedagogical approaches are addressed.
  • The local curriculum process addresses equity, accessibility, and UDL principles.
