Check My Cal-GETC Area

Choose the Cal-GETC area you are targeting, then check whether your Course Outline of Record makes that area’s requirements visible. This is a Cal-GETC readiness check, not a complete Title 5 or local curriculum audit. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

What this tool checks

Does your COR make the Cal-GETC case clearly?

This tool focuses on the evidence a Cal-GETC reviewer needs to see for the area you select. It also flags a few statewide COR and materials requirements that commonly affect review.

What it does not replace

It is not a full Title 5 compliance audit, a substitute for your local curriculum process, or a guarantee of CSU/UC approval.
