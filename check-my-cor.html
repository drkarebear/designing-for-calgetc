Read it like a reviewer

Check My COR

The final checklist will help faculty ask a simple question: can a reviewer actually see the Cal-GETC requirement in the official outline?

Scope & disciplinary focus

Does the COR clearly show what kind of course this is and the academic work students will do?

Objectives & content

Are the required concepts and abilities visible rather than implied?

Assignments & evaluation

Do representative assignments and evaluation methods demonstrate the level and type of work the area requires?

Readings & course materials

Do the listed materials support the breadth, rigor, and disciplinary expectations of the course?

Important

This is currently a page framework, not an official approval checklist. We should populate the final checklist directly from the current reviewer notes, Title 5/COR guidance, and applicable area standards.
