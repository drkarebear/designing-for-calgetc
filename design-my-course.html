Design My Course

This section will turn statewide requirements into practical course-design questions without treating reviewer guidance as if it were regulation.

1. Identify the governing layer

Start with the Cal-GETC area, then check whether a CCN template, C-ID descriptor, UC transferability rule, or other statewide requirement also applies.

2. Map the standard to the COR

Ask where the requirement is visible: description, objectives, content, assignments, methods of evaluation, readings, or another required COR element.

3. Make the rigor visible

A reviewer can only evaluate what the official outline actually shows. The detailed guides will identify where vague or boilerplate language can create problems.

4. Check locally before submission

Use statewide guidance alongside your college or district curriculum process and your articulation officer's expertise.
