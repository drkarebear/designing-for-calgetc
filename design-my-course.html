Design or Revise a Course

Move from “I have a course” to “the COR makes the statewide requirements visible” without collapsing Cal-GETC, CCN, C-ID, UC transferability, and local curriculum into one thing.

The design path

Work through the layers in this order.

The order matters because a course can satisfy one statewide framework without automatically satisfying another.

Check baseline Cal-GETC eligibility

Confirm CSU/UC transferability, appropriate units, academic/theoretical focus, and that the course is not a prohibited course type.

Identify every framework that applies

Check whether the course has a Common Course Numbering template, a C-ID descriptor, UC TCA requirements, or other statewide articulation expectations.

Choose the intended Cal-GETC area

Open the area-specific standard and identify the content, disciplinary focus, student abilities, assignments, and materials that must be visible.

Map the requirement into the COR

Do not rely on the catalog description alone. Ask where a reviewer will actually see the requirement in objectives, content, methods, assignments, evaluation, and readings.

Check instructional materials

Confirm that representative textbooks, OER, online texts, or lab manuals meet the current statewide submission guidance.

Run the COR reviewer check

Look for gaps, contradictions, vague lists, boilerplate, and assignments that fail to demonstrate the expected rigor.

Use your local curriculum and articulation process

Statewide guidance does not replace local approval workflows or the expertise of your articulation officer and curriculum committee.

Verify the published result in ASSIST

After statewide review, use ASSIST to confirm the approved area and effective term.

Keep the systems straight

Related does not mean interchangeable.

Cal-GETC

Lower-division general education criteria and certification areas.

COR

Your college's official course record describing the core components that apply across sections.

CCN

Common statewide course template elements that must be identical for aligned courses.

C-ID

Descriptor-based course comparability used heavily in TMC/ADT and some articulation.

UC TCA

Baseline UC transferability; TCA-approved courses may then be submitted for additional articulation or Cal-GETC.

ASSIST

The published statewide articulation record where Cal-GETC approvals and effective terms are displayed.

Sources: ICAS 2026 CCN articulation memo; ASCCC C-ID/CCN comparison; Cal-GETC Standards Version 1.4.
