Design or Revise a Course

Use this order to move from a course idea to a COR that clearly shows the Cal-GETC requirements.

The design path

Use this order.

A course can meet one framework without automatically meeting another.

Check baseline Cal-GETC eligibility

Confirm CSU/UC transferability, units, academic focus, and course type.

Identify every framework that applies

Check for a CCN template, C-ID descriptor, UC TCA criteria, or other statewide requirements.

Choose the intended Cal-GETC area

Open the area guide and identify what must be visible in the COR.

Map the requirement into the COR

Show the requirement in objectives, content, assignments, evaluation, and readings—not just the catalog description.

Check instructional materials

Check representative texts, OER, online materials, and any required lab manual.

Run the COR reviewer check

Look for gaps, contradictions, vague lists, boilerplate, and assignments that do not show the expected rigor.

Use your local curriculum and articulation process

Follow your college’s curriculum and articulation process.

Verify the published result in ASSIST

Confirm the approved area and effective term in ASSIST.

Need to untangle the acronyms?Reference
Cal-GETC

Lower-division general education criteria and certification areas.

COR

Your college's official course record describing the core components that apply across sections.

CCN

Common statewide course template elements that must be identical for aligned courses.

C-ID

Descriptor-based course comparability used heavily in TMC/ADT and some articulation.

UC TCA

Baseline UC transferability; TCA-approved courses may then be submitted for additional articulation or Cal-GETC.

ASSIST

The published statewide articulation record where Cal-GETC approvals and effective terms are displayed.

Sources: ICAS 2026 CCN articulation memo; ASCCC C-ID/CCN comparison; Cal-GETC Standards Version 1.4.
