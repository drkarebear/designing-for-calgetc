Gray Areas & FAQs

Some questions have a direct statewide answer. Others require faculty judgment, local process, or clarification from articulation experts. This page will say which is which.

Clearly answered by the standard

We will quote or closely paraphrase the governing source and link directly to it.

Reviewer guidance

We will identify guidance as reviewer guidance rather than presenting it as regulation.

Faculty design judgment

When multiple designs could satisfy the standard, examples will be framed as options rather than mandates.

Needs local confirmation

Local curriculum calendars, submission workflows, and institutional practices will be flagged as local rather than statewide.
