FAQs

Some questions have a direct statewide answer. Others require faculty judgment or local confirmation. This page says which kind of answer you are reading.

Does a CCN template replace my COR?

No. The 2026 ICAS memo says the COR remains the official CCC course document. CCN templates may support articulation when approved for that purpose, but CORs should continue to be maintained and revised.

Does being a CCN course automatically mean Cal-GETC approval?

No. CCN, baseline transferability, Cal-GETC, course-to-course articulation, and major preparation are distinct articulation layers. Check the applicable approval and ASSIST record.

Can one course be approved in more than one Cal-GETC area?

Yes, though reviewer guidance says it is not common and the course must holistically satisfy each area. For student certification, a course can ordinarily be applied to only one area.

Does a course description prove the course meets the area?

Not by itself. Reviewer guidance treats descriptions as comparatively less reliable and looks more heavily to objectives, content, instruction, evaluation, assignments, prerequisites, and materials.

Can OER be used?

Yes. Current standards accept stable, publicly available OER/online texts that function as published textbooks and are identified with title, author, and publication date.

Can a course be approved and later lose the area?

Yes. Existing approvals may be re-reviewed. If a course no longer meets the standard, it can be scheduled for phase-out while the college has time to revise and resubmit.
