Could this course qualify for Cal-GETC?4-question pre-check
✓
CSU and UC transferable?

Cal-GETC courses must be transferable to both public university systems.

✓
Enough units?

Most Cal-GETC courses are at least 3 semester or 4 quarter units; Area 5C and limited sequence exceptions differ.

✓
Academic rather than primarily practical or professional?

The course should be grounded in disciplinary concepts and modes of inquiry, not primarily personal, applied, skills, or professional training.

✓
A stable course rather than variable topics or independent study?

Special topics, variable topics, directed study, and independent study are not appropriate for Cal-GETC.

Source: Cal-GETC Standards Version 1.4, §§5.3–5.4.5. This is a screening aid, not an approval determination.

Version-aware

What changed recently?

Three changes that can affect course design now.

Version 1.4

Area 1A writing language changed

The current standard uses “5,000 words of scaffolded writing appropriate for academic contexts across major assignments.”

Version 1.4

Cross-listing must already be locally approved

Cross-listed CORs must be approved as such before submission and must align across specified COR elements.

Current review

Textbooks and OER are review evidence

Current standards specify how textbooks, OER, online texts, and lab manuals should be identified in the COR.
