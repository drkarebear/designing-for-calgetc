Sources

Every substantive page should let faculty move from plain-language guidance back to the current official source and know what kind of source they are reading.

ICAS · Cal-GETC Standards, Policies, and Procedures Version 1.4

Primary authority for Cal-GETC standards, course eligibility, approval processes, subject-area criteria, certification, units, grades, and exams.

Open the current ICAS standards page ↗ ↗ (external site)

ICAS · Version 1.4 Summary of Substantial Changes

Used for the Changes section and to distinguish current wording from prior versions.

Guiding Notes for Cal-GETC Course Review · 2025–2026 change summary

Reviewer-oriented guidance. The uploaded summary confirms that the notes are intended to show CCC colleagues what CSU/UC reviewers look for, while the official policy documents remain controlling.

Open current Guiding Notes ↗ ↗ (external site)

CCCCO · ESS 25-66, Regulatory Revisions to Course Outline of Record

Current implementation guidance for revised Title 5 COR requirements, including public accessibility, required COR elements, inclusive pedagogy, equity/accessibility, and UDL-related curriculum processes.

CCCCO · Program and Course Approval Handbook, 8th ed.

Background statewide curriculum guidance. Because ESS 25-66 anticipates a Ninth Edition, newer regulations and memos should control when they differ.

ICAS · May 15, 2026 CCN Templates and Articulation Memo

Explains the continuing role of the COR and the evolving role of CCN templates in baseline transferability and articulation.

ASCCC · C-ID and CCN Comparison

Useful for distinguishing descriptor-based C-ID from template-based CCN and their respective roles.

ASSIST · Understanding Year Terms in ASSIST

Defines Effective Begin and Effective End and helps interpret approval dates.

Open ASSIST ↗ ↗ (external site)

UCOP · TCA Review Checklist

Supports faculty/AO understanding of baseline UC transferability records, eligibility-area designations, phase-outs, and TCA review.
