CCCCO · ESS 25-66, Regulatory Revisions to Course Outline of Record

Current implementation guidance for revised Title 5 COR requirements, including public accessibility, required COR elements, inclusive pedagogy, equity/accessibility, and UDL-related curriculum processes.

CCCCO · Program and Course Approval Handbook, 8th ed.

Background statewide curriculum guidance. Because ESS 25-66 anticipates a Ninth Edition, newer regulations and memos should control when they differ.

ICAS · May 15, 2026 CCN Templates and Articulation Memo

Explains the continuing role of the COR and the evolving role of CCN templates in baseline transferability and articulation.

ASCCC · C-ID and CCN Comparison

Useful for distinguishing descriptor-based C-ID from template-based CCN and their respective roles.

ASSIST · Understanding Year Terms in ASSIST

Defines Effective Begin and Effective End and helps interpret approval dates.
